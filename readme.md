# Radar Interference: Sliced (C&I interference) Signal Modeling and Analysis [Includes MATLAB Code]

I. Basic Principles of Slice Interference
The principle of chopping interference (Choppin and Interference, C&I) is to utilize the coherent gain brought by matched filtering in pulse compression radar signal processing to enhance the suppression or deception effect of chopping interference. Its implementation approach involves digital radio frequency memory, direct digital synthesizer for undistorted sampling, storage, signal processing, and formation of analog signals before forwarding them. The forwarded interference signals are highly correlated with the radar signals, and after being filtered by a matched filter, the interference signals can form individual or multiple false targets.
